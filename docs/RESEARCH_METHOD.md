# Research method

## What counts as evidence

Each strategy begins as an explicit, falsifiable hypothesis: its universe, signal, holding/exit logic, decision time, parameters, benchmark, and cost/execution assumptions are recorded before evaluation. A profitable backtest is evidence requiring validation, not proof of a durable market edge.

Evidence should be reproducible from immutable dataset identity and provenance, configuration, code revision, seed where applicable, and structured outputs. Failed experiments, negative results, and known limitations are evidence too; they must not be silently discarded.

## Temporal discipline

Use chronological train/development/validation/final-holdout partitions appropriate to the hypothesis. Final holdout data is not used to choose parameters. Walk-forward evaluation should repeat the chronological process across successive windows when enough data exists.

Prevent look-ahead bias by modeling when each datum became available. P0.1 represents completed bars at interval end and records a separate UTC availability instant; later decision/simulation code must consume only records whose availability instant has arrived. Completed-bar strategies cannot assume knowledge of that bar's future path to obtain a same-bar fill. Address survivorship bias when the instrument universe can change over time. Preserve source timezone and session semantics as provenance throughout processing.

P0.2A makes the corresponding feature rule executable: each feature snapshot is available no earlier than the latest availability time of the bars required to compute it. Its trailing-window calculations are prefix-stable: appending later observations must leave every already-defined snapshot exactly unchanged. This protects feature research from accidental look-ahead before strategies or simulation exist; it does not by itself establish valid decision or fill timing.

P0.4A makes the initial execution-timing rule executable without modeling a fill: a P0.3 signal becomes pending at its explicit availability/decision time and may be eligible only at the first same-instrument execution opportunity whose modeled event time is strictly later. Equal timestamps are never sufficient, even though event processing has a fixed causal order. In particular, no completed-bar close used to create the feature and signal can also be its execution opportunity. Upstream delayed availability remains authoritative, and timeline traces are prefix-stable when future events are appended.

P0.4B makes the resulting lifecycle state executable without adding economic execution semantics. The dedicated lifecycle component, rather than a strategy, feature engine, or timeline, authoritatively owns one isolated state per instrument: `flat`, `pending_entry`, `holding`, or `pending_exit`. A P0.4A-eligible later opportunity may open or close that state but is not a fill-price claim. Its immutable trace records prior state, event, decision, reason, resulting state, and related action/position evidence. Redundant or impossible signals are explicit non-actions, same-instant opposing entry/exit proposals reject both, and pending/open final states remain visible rather than being silently canceled or liquidated. The lifecycle records `opened_at` for a future modeled holding-horizon policy.

P0.4C composes these components in one run without changing their authority. It evaluates the initial hypothesis once per configured instrument and feature-availability instant. When delayed delivery makes multiple historical snapshots available together, it selects the newest observation rather than creating duplicate backfill actions. Although P0.2 can calculate immutable prefix-stable snapshots from the complete validated delivery, P0.4C does not precompute strategy outcomes: it advances decisions by availability, supplies only P0.4B state established strictly before that time, then emits a P0.3 signal if the existing strategy proposes one. Thus a later snapshot cannot reach an earlier decision, and feature/signal equality at a timestamp cannot convert the subsequent same-time opportunity into a legal transition. Pending lifecycle states fail closed to no new strategy action. The current hypothesis has a threshold exit but no maximum-holding configuration; P0.4C explicitly defers a horizon rather than treating elapsed time or eligible-bar count as an unstated rule.

The resulting timeline/lifecycle trace is structural evidence only. P0.4C's admission of a signal to the research lifecycle is a temporary simulation assumption until P2's independent risk authority exists. An execution opportunity still does not establish a broker fill, executable price, liquidity, or profitability. Prefix comparisons retain historical features, signals, event order, eligibility, and transitions when later observations are appended; only unresolved end state can receive a later structural resolution.

## Falsification and robustness

Treat parameter mining and multiple testing as threats to evidence: record alternatives considered and avoid presenting the best discovered configuration as an independent result. Evaluate explicit transaction costs, sensitivity to plausible slippage, latency, and execution constraints. Compare against relevant benchmarks, inspect parameter stability, and use ablations to identify which assumptions or components create the result.

Report sample size and uncertainty honestly. Small samples, rare regimes, and concentrated outcomes can make conventional-looking metrics misleading. Research claims must state the limitations of the data and model rather than imply certainty. Temporal correctness, deterministic behavior, and risk authority are documented requirements now and must receive executable regression or property tests when their implementations are introduced.

P0.3 records the first unvalidated hypothesis without evaluating it: a sufficiently negative completed-close z-score may be followed by short-horizon mean reversion. Its thresholds, window, feature identity/version, data-freshness allowance, direction, and strategy/configuration identities are explicit typed inputs. The current contract deliberately has no maximum-holding value because no simulator owns authoritative holding elapsed time yet. No parameter search or claim of profitability occurred; P0.4/P0.5 and P1 evaluation remain necessary for falsification.
