# Research method

## What counts as evidence

Each strategy begins as an explicit, falsifiable hypothesis: its universe, signal, holding/exit logic, decision time, parameters, benchmark, and cost/execution assumptions are recorded before evaluation. A profitable backtest is evidence requiring validation, not proof of a durable market edge.

Evidence should be reproducible from immutable dataset identity and provenance, configuration, code revision, seed where applicable, and structured outputs. Failed experiments, negative results, and known limitations are evidence too; they must not be silently discarded.

## Temporal discipline

Use chronological train/development/validation/final-holdout partitions appropriate to the hypothesis. Final holdout data is not used to choose parameters. Walk-forward evaluation should repeat the chronological process across successive windows when enough data exists.

Prevent look-ahead bias by modeling when each datum became available. P0.1 represents completed bars at interval end and records a separate UTC availability instant; later decision/simulation code must consume only records whose availability instant has arrived. Completed-bar strategies cannot assume knowledge of that bar's future path to obtain a same-bar fill. Address survivorship bias when the instrument universe can change over time. Preserve source timezone and session semantics as provenance throughout processing.

P0.2A makes the corresponding feature rule executable: each feature snapshot is available no earlier than the latest availability time of the bars required to compute it. Its trailing-window calculations are prefix-stable: appending later observations must leave every already-defined snapshot exactly unchanged. This protects feature research from accidental look-ahead before strategies or simulation exist; it does not by itself establish valid decision or fill timing.

P0.4A makes the initial execution-timing rule executable without modeling a fill: a P0.3 signal establishes a strict same-instrument eligibility boundary at its explicit availability/decision time. An execution opportunity must be strictly later; equal timestamps are never sufficient, even though event processing has a fixed causal order. In particular, no completed-bar close used to create the feature and signal can also be its execution opportunity. A legal opportunity is permission to attempt, not evidence that execution happened. Upstream delayed availability remains authoritative, and timeline traces are prefix-stable when future events are appended.

P0.4B makes the resulting lifecycle state executable without adding economic execution semantics. The dedicated lifecycle component, rather than a strategy, feature engine, or timeline, authoritatively owns one isolated state per instrument: `flat`, `pending_entry`, `holding`, or `pending_exit`. P0.5A refines its temporary structural simplification: a P0.4A-eligible opportunity may create an attempt, but only an explicit `filled` outcome may open or close state. Its immutable trace records prior state, event, decision, reason, resulting state, related action/position, and execution evidence. Redundant or impossible signals are explicit non-actions, same-instant opposing entry/exit proposals reject both, and pending/open final states remain visible rather than being silently canceled or liquidated. The lifecycle records `opened_at` for a future modeled holding-horizon policy.

P0.4C composes these components in one run without changing their authority. It evaluates the initial hypothesis once per configured instrument and feature-availability instant. When delayed delivery makes multiple historical snapshots available together, it selects the newest observation rather than creating duplicate backfill actions. Although P0.2 can calculate immutable prefix-stable snapshots from the complete validated delivery, P0.4C does not precompute strategy outcomes: it advances decisions by availability, supplies only P0.4B state established strictly before that time, then emits a P0.3 signal if the existing strategy proposes one. Thus a later snapshot cannot reach an earlier decision, and feature/signal equality at a timestamp cannot convert the subsequent same-time opportunity into a legal transition. Pending lifecycle states fail closed to no new strategy action. The current hypothesis has a threshold exit but no maximum-holding configuration; P0.4C explicitly defers a horizon rather than treating elapsed time or eligible-bar count as an unstated rule.

P0.5A introduces the narrowest executable-price baseline using P0.1 `Quote` evidence. A quote can influence an attempt only when its `availability_time` has arrived; the newest causal matching quote wins, with deterministic stable-reference tie-breaking. Its market age is separately bounded by `attempt_time - observation_time`. Long entries buy ask and long exits sell bid, so the observed bid/ask spread is retained without subtracting an invented additional spread cost. A locked quote is usable under the P0.1 contract; crossed quote evidence rejects the attempt rather than being repaired. Missing, delayed, or stale evidence is unfilled. No price is selected by midpoint, completed-bar close, future evidence, or favorable forward search.

P0.5A's `filled` outcome is execution-model evidence, not economic evaluation: it adds no quantity, commission, slippage, liquidity assertion, cash, P&L, return, or profitability claim. `unfilled` and `rejected` attempts keep the lifecycle action pending under the declared policy. Prefix comparisons retain historical features, signals, event order, eligibility, attempts, outcomes, and transitions when future observations, quotes, or opportunities are appended; only an unresolved end state can receive a later attempt or resolution. P0.4C/P0.5A admission of a signal to the research lifecycle remains a temporary simulation assumption until P2's independent risk authority exists.

## Falsification and robustness

Treat parameter mining and multiple testing as threats to evidence: record alternatives considered and avoid presenting the best discovered configuration as an independent result. Evaluate explicit transaction costs, sensitivity to plausible slippage, latency, and execution constraints. Compare against relevant benchmarks, inspect parameter stability, and use ablations to identify which assumptions or components create the result.

Report sample size and uncertainty honestly. Small samples, rare regimes, and concentrated outcomes can make conventional-looking metrics misleading. Research claims must state the limitations of the data and model rather than imply certainty. Temporal correctness, deterministic behavior, and risk authority are documented requirements now and must receive executable regression or property tests when their implementations are introduced.

P0.3 records the first unvalidated hypothesis without evaluating it: a sufficiently negative completed-close z-score may be followed by short-horizon mean reversion. Its thresholds, window, feature identity/version, data-freshness allowance, direction, and strategy/configuration identities are explicit typed inputs. The current contract deliberately has no maximum-holding value because no simulator owns authoritative holding elapsed time yet. No parameter search or claim of profitability occurred; P0.4/P0.5 and P1 evaluation remain necessary for falsification.

P0.5B adds a caller-declared deterministic adverse slippage assumption after P0.5A quote eligibility and side selection. It preserves the exact quote and baseline price, records per-outcome configuration and modeled price, and supplies canonical ascending-bps sensitivity outcomes. Zero is the exact P0.5A price baseline; positive assumptions never improve price and higher assumptions weakly worsen it. Fixed Decimal arithmetic, explicit numeric-domain limits, failed-evidence preservation, retries, and historical simulation-prefix regressions bound this claim. These scenarios are price-level falsification fixtures, not actual-market slippage estimates or strategy-performance evidence.

P0.5C attaches a caller-declared fixed positive quantity and explicit nonnegative proportional fee to each filled outcome after its final P0.5B price is known. It uses absolute executed notional as the fee base, so fees remain nonnegative even for zero/negative stress prices, and derives only the individual execution cash flow. The quantity is an experiment assumption with multiplier one, not strategy or risk sizing; the declared currency is a research denomination, not verified instrument metadata or an FX model. Fractional units are a research-domain abstraction and imply no venue support. The synthetic fee is not a broker schedule. Unfilled/rejected outcomes have no economic execution, and changes to quantity, currency, or fee cannot affect upstream signals, fills, prices, retries, or lifecycle state. Prefix and hostile-Decimal-context regressions bound this claim.

P0.5 is therefore complete only as the bounded composition of quote-side executable pricing, deterministic adverse slippage, fixed declared quantity, and proportional fee evidence. It does not establish realistic liquidity, partial fills, impact, market feasibility, broker behavior, portfolio accounting, or profitability. P1A may next reconstruct entry/exit fills into trades and establish the first historical-evaluation foundation; it must keep zero/negative stress evidence explicit rather than silently treating it as an ordinary observation.
